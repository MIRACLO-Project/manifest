# Test checklist

Run through this on a clean flash (format data) and on a dirty flash from the previous build
before a release. Note the build number, the variant (GApps or Vanilla) and anything that fails.

## First boot and setup
- [ ] Boots to Mira Setup; Hello, Make it yours, Controls and Liquid Glass all respond.
- [ ] Light or Dark sets the theme and the matching Aurora wallpaper on home and lock screen.
- [ ] Gestures or Buttons takes effect; the three buttons sit centred, not squeezed left.
- [ ] The chosen Quick Settings style is in use after setup.
- [ ] Google's (or Android's) setup follows; Wi-Fi, account and restore work.

## Calls, data and connectivity
- [ ] Calls both ways on both SIMs; VoLTE and VoWiFi; SMS and MMS.
- [ ] Mobile data, Wi-Fi (2.4 and 5 GHz), hotspot (WPA2 and WPA3), Bluetooth audio, NFC (where fitted).
- [ ] Call recording: Record from the notification; the file plays back from Recordings › Call recordings.

## Display, sound and sensors
- [ ] 120 Hz in use; Adaptive drops to 60 Hz when idle; per-app refresh rate applies and restores.
- [ ] Extra dim; always-on display (Always, Scheduled, Tap to show); edge lighting on a new message.
- [ ] Speaker, earpiece, headset; Mira Dolby on and off, profiles, equalizer.
- [ ] Fingerprint, proximity during calls, auto-brightness, auto-rotate, pocket mode.

## System UI
- [ ] All four Quick Settings styles, with and without Liquid Glass, in light and dark.
- [ ] Notification styles, spacing, transparency, blur; lock screen Count, Stack and List.
- [ ] Volume panel, app volumes, Dynamic Island (media, calls, timers, charging).
- [ ] Text is readable everywhere in Quick Settings and the shade over a bright wallpaper.

## Mira Studio, launcher and apps
- [ ] Every Mira Studio page opens and every option takes effect (and survives a reboot).
- [ ] Launcher: dock, folders, drawer, icon packs, recents layouts, App lock hides recents previews.
- [ ] Mira GameSpace: game assistant over a game, performance mode, settings restored after the game.
- [ ] Camera: photos and video on every lens, front camera, flash.

## Updates
- [ ] Mira Updater checks, downloads (pause and resume), installs and restarts.
- [ ] The OTA zip installs from recovery over the previous build.

## Battery and performance
- [ ] Idle drain overnight; standby with Wi-Fi and data.
- [ ] No overheating during a 30-minute game; no stutter in the launcher, shade and recents.
